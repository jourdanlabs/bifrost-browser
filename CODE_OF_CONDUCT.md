# Community Standards

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate with respect, focus criticism on the work, protect private user data, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexual harassment, and sustained disruption are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive material and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s maintainer contact or GitHub reporting tools.
